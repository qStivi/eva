Eva · local brain evaluation · 14 models

Which local model should be Eva?

A persona-first bake-off on the RX 9070 XT (16 GB, ROCm/GGUF). Every model met Eva's real system prompt and an 8-prompt battery — voice, boundaries, reasoning, tool-intent, format, memory, German, and the emotional-deflection beat — judged on being Eva, not just answering. The bar to beat: her current brain, gpt-oss-20b.

Headline

Three small models — Ministral 3 (3B & 14B) and the DeepSeek R1-distill 8B — beat the baseline on persona while matching it on reasoning. Ministral-3-3B doing this at 3B / 88 tok/s is the surprise. The reasoning-tuned newcomers (Phi-4, Hunyuan, MiMo) leak their thinking and describe Eva instead of being her.

The engine wrinkle

Updating LM Studio's runtime to test the crash-on-load models surfaced a hard fork: the two backends are mutually exclusive for the models that matter.

▸ Vulkan 2.28.2 — kept

  • Runs gpt-oss-20b (Eva's brain) + every leading candidate
  • Crashes qwen3-30b, gemma-4, lfm2-24b
  • Reverted here — the safe home; all finalists ran on it

▸ ROCm 2.28.2 — not worth it

  • Unlocks qwen3-30b (good voice, but 23 tok/s)
  • Breaks gpt-oss-20b (SIGABRT) — can't run Eva's current brain
  • gemma-4 still needs a flash-attention config fix
